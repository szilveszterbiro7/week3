##  About the Game

The Last Guest is a short, atmospheric horror game where you explore an abandoned hotel, uncover its secrets, and try to escape before it's too late.

What starts as a simple exploration quickly turns into a race against time.

The hotel seems empty, but something is watching you.

And it doesn't want you to leave.

##  Story

You arrive at a hotel.

The reception is empty. The corridors are silent. An old bell sits on the counter, but nobody answers when you ring it.

While exploring, you discover a mysterious key that unlocks Room 6.

Inside, you find a letter.

Suddenly, a shadow appears.

The hotel is no longer safe.

##  Gameplay

Explore the hotel, interact with objects, collect clues, and discover what happened inside its walls.

Your goal is to find the key to Room 6, investigate the mysterious room, and escape the hotel before time runs out.
<img width="637" height="891" alt="Képernyőkép 2026-11-09 012203" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/df1fe0f8-15cd-4c9a-88c7-ae3bce7b7796" />

### Features

-  2D Pixel Art: A classic 2D horror atmosphere.
-  Hotel Exploration: Explore the reception, corridors, and mysterious rooms.
-  Interactive Objects: Examine letters and other objects.
-  Mysterious Figure: Encounter a strange figure hiding inside the hotel.
-  Timed Escape: You have only 10 seconds to escape after triggering the chase.
-  Horror Effects: Chase music, flashing red effects, and unsettling messages.
-  Multiple Endings: Your actions determine whether you escape or get caught.

##  Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W / ↑ | Move up |
| S / ↓ | Move down |
| A / ← | Move left |
| D / → | Move right |
| E | Interact with objects, doors, and letters |

##  How to Play
You can play by clicking this link: [last_guest](https://spagettimaffia.itch.io/last-guest)

##  Endings

The game has two possible endings:

**1. Escaped Ending**

You successfully reach the hotel's exit before the countdown reaches zero.

**2. Caught Ending**

You fail to escape within the time limit, and the mysterious shadow catches you.
